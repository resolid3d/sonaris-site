Audiobooks from your phone — no accounts, no ads

Sonaris plays the audiobooks already on your phone: MP3, M4A, M4B, Opus. It remembers where you stopped and lets you fall asleep to a book without losing your place.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

Library from a folderEach subfolder is a book. Series group themselves; M4B chapters come from the file.
Sleep timerFades the sound out, and in the morning rewinds to where you fell asleep.
Speed and sound per bookEach book keeps its own speed, pitch and equalizer.
Rewind after pauseThe longer the pause, the further back — so you don’t lose the thread.
Bookmarks and historyBookmarks with notes, listening history and stats.
Widget and headsetControl from the home screen, the notification and headphone buttons.

Privacy

Everything stays on your phone. The app goes online only when you search for a cover or description. Details are in the privacy policy.
